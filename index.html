How to Play

Overview

Stronghold Quest is a card game where you defend innocent life by drawing Challenge Cards, answering questions, and overcoming Threats. Collect 7 cards to win, or 12 for a greater victory!

Setup

Choose a Pillar (Purity, Protection, Peace, or Productivity) to guide your mission. Each pillar represents a different aspect of defending life.

Each Turn

  • Draw a Challenge Card – Read the challenge, then answer a teaching question to collect it.
  • Every 3rd turn: Threat! – A Threat Card appears. Answer correctly to defeat it, or lose a collected card.
  • Earn EZ Merit Points – Each card collected or threat defeated earns merit.

Victory

  • 7 cards – Standard victory: claim your Defender of Life badge!
  • 12 cards – Greater victory: you are a true champion of life!

Prayer

Tap the prayer button at any time to pray for life. Prayers follow the Liturgy of the Hours, matching the time of day. Each prayer earns 1 EZ Merit Point.

EZ Merit Points

Log in with your Sentinel Ops account to earn EZ Merit Points. Points earned in Stronghold Quest count toward your total across all Zoseco games (Stellar Virtue, StagQuest, and more).
